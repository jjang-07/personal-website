Jun Jang

Researcher, builder, and musician at Stanford

Hi, I'm Jun.

I'm a Stanford undergrad (Class of 2030) studying computer science. I'm drawn to NLP and computational linguistics, interpretable AI, and education. I grew up in rural Mississippi, and a lot of what I build traces back there.

In high school I built AI Ink, a tool that helps K–12 teachers flag AI-written essays, and grew it into an authorship verification system. That work led to research at the University of Mississippi, first-author papers at venues including AAAI and CSCI, and two trips to Regeneron ISEF as a finalist.

When I'm not doing research, I'm making music. I also coded for my VEX Robotics team, led my school's speech and debate team, and founded Magnolia Coding Club, an online coding community for rural students across ten Mississippi schools.
